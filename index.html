A note, just for you

Dear Akshu,

There are certain people who make ordinary moments feel a little more beautiful and I think you are one of them.

I don't know how to put everything into words but there is something incredibly special about the way certain souls make us feel. It's in the smallest things, the unexpected smiles, the silly conversations, the comfortable silences and those little moments that somehow become memories we carry with us.

I hope life is gentle with you. I hope you find reasons to smile even on the days that feel particularly heavy. I hope you never lose the little things that make you who you are, your dreams, your laughter, your kindness and that beautiful spark within you.

If there is one thing I would want you to know, it is that you deserve happiness in its simplest and purest form. Not just the grand, extraordinary kind, but the everyday kind that makes waking up feel worthwhile.

And perhaps, somewhere between all the ordinary days and extraordinary moments, life will always remind you that you are someone worth appreciating.

Take care of yourself, always.
